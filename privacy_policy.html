Privacy Policy - Idle Clicker Industry

Effective date: 10 October 2026
Developer: Arbin Bici, Albania
Contact: arbinbici00@gmail.com

1. The short version

Idle Clicker Industry is a single-player game. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no server of ours. Your progress stays on your phone. The game shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch them) and sells optional in-app purchases. Those two features are provided by Google, and the game checks the time over the internet to stop clock tampering. That is all the data that leaves your device, and this policy lists it.

2. What the game stores on your device

  • Your save: buildings, upgrades, research, currencies, statistics, achievements, and timers (such as boost and cooldown timers). It is stored in the app's private storage and is not sent to us.
  • Your settings: volumes, switches and the number format.
  • A time record: the last verified time, used to work out how long the game was closed. It is kept in a separate private file.
  • Cached purchase status: whether you own "Remove ads", the production booster or the starter pack. Google Play is the source of truth and is checked every time the game starts.

If you use Android's backup, Android may copy the save and settings to your Google account and restore them on a new phone. The time record is excluded from backups. Uninstalling the game deletes the local data.

3. What the game sends over the internet

  1. Time check. To keep boosts, research timers and offline earnings fair, the game asks a few large public websites for the current time (the Date header of a normal HTTPS response): www.google.com, www.cloudflare.com and www.microsoft.com. These sites see your IP address and the usual technical details of any web request. The game sends nothing about you or your game.
  2. Ads (Google AdMob). Rewarded ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and operating system information, app information, and information about how you interact with ads, and may use cookies or similar technologies. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where it applies, the game first shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) and does not request ads until you have made your choice; you can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings. See Google's description of how it uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites Ads are limited to content suitable for teens (maximum content rating "T"). If you buy Remove ads, the game no longer asks for ads.
  3. Purchases (Google Play Billing). Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. The game asks Google Play which products you own so it can unlock them.

The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, does not read your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera, and does not use accounts. Local notifications (for example "your research is complete") are scheduled on your device and need the notification permission, which you can refuse or withdraw in Android settings.

4. How the data is used

Time check: to prevent changing the phone's date from skipping timers. Ads: to show rewarded ads and measure them (Google). Purchases: to deliver what you bought (Google). We do not sell personal data and we do not build profiles.

5. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. Ads are limited to a "T" content rating. If you believe a child has used the game with ads enabled, tell us at the address above and we will help.

6. Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you on our own servers, there is nothing for us to export or delete; clearing the game's storage or uninstalling removes the local data. For data held by Google (advertising, Play purchases) use your Google Account's privacy controls (https://myaccount.google.com) and Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK under the GDPR or California under the CCPA) you may have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection with respect to data controllers; for the data our partners hold, contact them; for anything else, contact us.

7. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the game.
